Questions

Is the result official?

No. Kinlevel calculates an estimate from your answers using the criteria published in law. The official decision is made by the assessing body (Medizinischer Dienst, DWP, Conseil départemental) and can differ. Kinlevel is independent and not affiliated with any government agency, insurer or assessment service.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There are no accounts, no servers and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Which sources does Kinlevel use?

Germany: SGB XI § 15 with Anlage 1 and 2 (gesetze-im-internet.de). UK: The Social Security (Personal Independence Payment) Regulations 2013, Schedule 1 (legislation.gov.uk). France: grille AGGIR, Code de l’action sociale et des familles, annexe 2-1. The sources are also listed in the app under Settings → About.

Is the appeal letter legal advice?

No. It is a general template you fill in and check yourself. For advice, contact a care advice centre (Pflegestützpunkt, Pflegeberatung), Citizens Advice, or your local information centre (CLIC).

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app.
