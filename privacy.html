Privacy Policy · last updated 23 November 2026 · Deutsch · Français

Short version: Everything you enter stays on your device. Kinlevel has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your answers, your care diary or any health information.

Who is responsible

Kinlevel is made by Christian Effner, Breitlacherstraße 86, 60489 Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Contact: christian.effner@googlemail.com.

What you enter

Profiles (a name or nickname and the relationship), your answers to the assessment questions, notes, care diary entries, checklist ticks and the details you type into the letter template are saved only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are included in your regular device backup if you use one, and are deleted when you choose Settings → Delete all data or delete the app. This information can concern health and care needs; it never leaves your device unless you share it yourself.

PDFs you create

The briefing PDF and the appeal letter are created on your device only when you ask for them. You decide whether to print, save or share them, and with whom, using the system’s share sheet.

No third parties, no network

Kinlevel contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools and does not connect to any server of ours. Links to official sources open in your browser only when you tap them.

Not an official decision, not advice

Kinlevel shows an estimate based on criteria published in law. It is not legal or medical advice and not an official decision. Kinlevel is independent and not affiliated with any government agency, insurer or assessment service.

Your rights

Because we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete — all your data is under your control on your device. Questions: christian.effner@googlemail.com. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes

If Kinlevel ever starts processing data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released.
